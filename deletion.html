User Data Deletion Instructions

Effective Date: September 5, 2026

Omnicast Automation is a personal automation application designed for direct content management. We respect your data privacy and comply with the Platform Data Deletion requirements of Meta (Facebook) and other supported platforms.

1. Data Storage Policy

Omnicast Automation operates as a local client tool. We do not save, host, or store personal user data on external servers or databases. All interactions occur directly between the authorized user and the official platform APIs (such as Meta Graph API / YouTube API).

2. How to Remove the App and Delete Your Data

If you have authorized Omnicast Automation on your Facebook account and wish to revoke access and delete any associated activity, follow these steps:

  1. Go to your Facebook profile's Settings & Privacy > Settings.
  2. In the left menu, click on Apps and Websites.
  3. Find Omnicast Automation in your list of connected apps.
  4. Click the Remove button next to the app.
  5. (Optional) Check the box to allow Facebook to delete all posts, videos, or events published on your behalf, then click Remove to confirm.

3. Requesting Manual Data Deletion

If you wish to formally request verification or deletion of any temporary local metadata or cache associated with your account, you can contact the developer directly:

  • Contact via GitHub: bennv14

Upon receiving your request, any local tokens or temporary logs associated with your account will be permanently deleted within 48 hours.
