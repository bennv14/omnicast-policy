Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 5, 2026

1. Introduction

The Omnicast Automation Tool ("we", "our", "us") is a private application used exclusively by its developer to upload and manage content across their own social media channels (e.g., YouTube, Facebook). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains our data practices regarding the use of platform APIs.

2. Data Collection and Usage

Because this tool is strictly for personal use by the developer, we do not collect, store, or share personal data from any third-party users.

When the developer uses the tool to connect to third-party APIs (such as the Google / YouTube Data API, Meta Graph API, etc.), the tool processes data solely for the purpose of uploading video files and metadata to the developer's authorized channels. It does not collect data from anyone else.

3. Use of API Services

The application's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Specifically, the tool requires scopes like youtube.upload to automatically upload videos on behalf of the developer. No data obtained from Google or any other social platform is shared, sold, or transferred to any third party.

4. Third-Party Services

We use various APIs for content distribution. By using those API services, this tool is bound by the respective platforms' terms, including the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via GitHub.
